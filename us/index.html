How to Play

A starting city sits at the compass center. Your mission: rotate the compass needle to point to the target city.

You have one chance. No take-backs!

The Challenge

The bearing is a rhumb line heading — the constant compass direction you'd follow to travel from one city to the other on a standard map.

See how your navigation skills compare to some famous (fictional) explorers.
