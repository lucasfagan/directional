    How to play

    Each day you get one patient. The case unfolds over 5–7 decision points: what to order, what to give, what to do when things change. It's Step 3 CCS–style management, condensed.

    Some questions want the single best next step. Others are select all that apply: pick everything you'd actually do, and nothing you wouldn't.

    Scoring

    🟩Best answer, or every key item with no extras 🟨Acceptable, or most of the key items 🟧Not ideal: wrong, or missing most key items 🟥Harmful choice

    On select-all questions, unnecessary picks cost a little and harmful ones cost a lot. Your score is out of 100.

    Educational game for medical trainees. Not medical advice. Doses and thresholds follow common US guidelines but simplify real practice.
