    How to play

    It's a Step 3 CCS-style case. Nobody tells you what to do. You get a patient and a clock, and you write orders.

    Controls

    • Type an order and pick it from the list. Orders take effect immediately; results take time (a fingerstick takes minutes, a culture takes a day).
    • ⏩ Clock moves time forward. "Next result" skips to when the next result is ready. The clock stops early if the nurse calls or the patient changes.
    • 🩺 Exam and 💬 Talk (interval history) cost a few minutes each.
    • 📋 Orders shows what's running and what's pending. Stop or change infusions there.
    • 🏥 Move the patient between ED, ward, and ICU, or discharge home.
    • 🏁 End the case when you're done, and name the diagnosis.

    Scoring

    As in CCS, you're graded on key actions and their timing, plus points off for harmful orders and a small deduction for unnecessary ones. Don't forget monitoring, location, and the boring stuff: prophylaxis, counseling, follow-up.

    Share squares: 🟩 key action done on time · 🟨 partial or late · ⬜ missed.

    Educational game for medical trainees. Not medical advice. The physiology is a simplified model; doses and thresholds follow common US guidelines.
