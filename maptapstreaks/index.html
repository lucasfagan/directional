How to play

The game

Each round names a country. Find it on the satellite globe — there are no borders and no labels, just the planet.

Keep going as long as you can. One miss ends your run, and your score is how many you got in a row.

Everyone gets the same countries in the same order. A new puzzle drops every day at midnight UTC.

Guessing

Press & hold (default): hold your finger still on a spot and a ring fills up — when it completes, that's your guess. Dragging or pinching cancels it, so you can explore freely.

Tap + confirm: tap to drop a pin, then hit Confirm. Switch modes in ⚙ Settings.

Tiny countries and island nations have a little wiggle room — get close and it counts.

Difficulty

Easy — big or unmistakable
Medium — mid-size, findable
Hard — small or tricky

The run starts gentle and gets harder as you go. Your share grid shows the difficulty of every country you found.
